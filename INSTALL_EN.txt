NothingOS 5.0 (Nothing Phone 3, Android 17) for Pixel 9 Pro XL (komodo)
=======================================================================
Port by plus_minus. Use at your own risk: unlocked bootloader, full data wipe.
Back up your data first.

REQUIREMENTS
- Pixel 9 Pro XL (komodo) with an UNLOCKED bootloader.
- Recent Google platform-tools (fastboot 35+).
- Pixel factory image komodo BP1A.250505.005 (Android 15, May 2025):
  https://developers.google.com/android/images#komodo
  The port runs on top of this exact vendor build; other builds are untested.

INSTALL
1. Flash stock BP1A.250505.005 (factory flash-all or https://flash.android.com).
   Boot it once to make sure it works, then power off.
2. Enter bootloader (Power + Vol Down), connect USB.
3. Extract the port archive into its own folder.
4. Linux/macOS:  ./flash-all.sh
   Windows:      flash-all.bat   (fastboot.exe must be in PATH or in this folder)
   The script:
     - checks the device is komodo on the expected bootloader;
     - flashes vendor_boot.img and vbmeta.img (with verity/verification disabled);
     - reboots to fastbootd and flashes product, system, system_ext;
     - offers a data wipe (REQUIRED when coming from stock);
     - reboots.
5. First boot takes 3-5 minutes.

MANUAL INSTALL (same as the script)
  fastboot flash vendor_boot vendor_boot.img
  fastboot flash --disable-verity --disable-verification vbmeta vbmeta.img
  fastboot reboot fastboot
  fastboot flash product product.img
  fastboot flash system system.img
  fastboot flash system_ext system_ext.img
  fastboot -w
  fastboot reboot

TROUBLESHOOTING
- "Not enough space to resize partition": the other slot's partitions fill the
  super partition. In fastbootd run `fastboot getvar current-slot`, then delete
  the OTHER slot's partitions (example for current slot b):
    fastboot delete-logical-partition product_a
    fastboot delete-logical-partition system_a
    fastboot delete-logical-partition system_ext_a
  and flash product/system/system_ext again.
- Bootloop: wipe again (fastboot -w). If that doesn't help, go back to stock.

BACK TO STOCK
  Flash factory image BP1A.250505.005 with wipe (Google flash-all or
  flash.android.com). This restores vendor_boot, vbmeta and all system partitions.

NOTES
- Camera: Pixel Camera (GCam) is built in, with all lenses and EdgeTPU.
  Nothing Camera is removed (it does not work with the Pixel camera HAL).
- No face unlock.
- VoLTE/VoWiFi untested.
- No root included. KernelSU/Magisk install as usual (patch init_boot from
  the BP1A.250505.005 factory image).
